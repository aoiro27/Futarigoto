## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished iOS app icon image for a warm Japanese couples/family reflection app, Futarigoto. 1024x1024 square, full bleed opaque background, no text or lettering. Design: two adorable rounded little mochi-like characters nestling together under a simple coral-peach house roof, conveying two people caring for their home. One character warm buttercream, one soft lavender, dark plum tiny eyes and soft smiles, little peach blush cheeks. A single small coral heart above/between them. Bold, generously sized central silhouette filling about 75% of canvas, easily recognizable at 60px. Premium cute Japanese stationery aesthetic, playful yet tasteful and grown-up, soft rounded shapes, clean flat illustration with very subtle tactile paper texture, no heavy outlines, no glossy 3D. Background a warm pale peach #FFE0D4; accents lavender #E9E1F8, mint #DDEEDF, warm cream #FFF9F3, deep plum #494356 for eyes. Clear separation between foreground and background. Two tiny flower/sparkle accents at most, avoid clutter. Entire square must be colored edge to edge: no pre-rounded icon corners, no outer white margin, no frame, no phone mockup, no drop shadow outside icon, no typography, no watermark. Make the roof and two faces feel like a unified distinctive friendly emblem.
